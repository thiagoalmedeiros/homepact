# Mortgage estimate: what your buyers see

In their proposal room, buyers see **Estimate your payments** under the price. They can try a down
payment, an interest rate, an amortization and a payment frequency, and see what they would pay
each period, without leaving the site. Nothing they type is sent to you or stored.

## What it shows

- **The posted 5-year rate** published by the Bank of Canada, with its trend over the last 12
  months. It is the banks' posted rate: lenders often offer less, so buyers can type their own.
- **The price, sales tax included**, from the current revision of the proposal.
- **The minimum down payment**: 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% above; 20% from $1.5 million.
  Below it, the estimate is greyed out with a warning.
- **Mortgage insurance** when the down payment is under 20%: 2.80% to 4.00% of the loan (0.20%
  more beyond 25 years), added to the mortgage. In Ontario, Quebec and Saskatchewan, the provincial
  sales tax on the premium is shown apart: it is paid at closing.
- **The payment**, the mortgage amount and the interest over the amortization. Accelerated
  bi-weekly payments show how much sooner the mortgage is paid off.

Every estimate says it is **not a financing offer**. It appears for companies that price in
Canadian dollars.

## Good to know

- If the Bank of Canada can't be reached, the last known rate is shown; if there is none yet,
  buyers type a rate themselves. The page always works.
- The insurance and down payment rules are the 2025 federal rules. When they change, the platform
  team updates them; until then estimates follow the previous rules.
- Have your legal contact confirm the wording if you have questions about financing disclosures,
  especially in Quebec.
